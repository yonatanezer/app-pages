Squishy Hole – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

Summary

Squishy Hole does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars), your Album, and your settings (sound, music, haptics, speed) are saved only on your device (local storage). We never receive them. Removing the app, clearing its data, or using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes them.

Advertising

This version of the game shows no ads and does not load any advertising SDK. If rewarded ads are ever enabled in a future update, they will be configured for child-directed treatment (non-personalized, G-rated content), and this policy and the store privacy labels will be updated first.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and has no chat, social features, or links that leave the game.

Web version

The web version runs entirely in your browser. It may store your progress in your browser's local storage and may cache the game files (service worker) so it can work offline. No cookies are used for tracking.

Third parties

The game uses open-source libraries (see the project's licence notes). None of them receives personal data from the game.

Contact

Developer: Tambal Studio · Contact: hallow@tambal.app

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the new version will be published at this address.
