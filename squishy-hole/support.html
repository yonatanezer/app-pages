Squishy Hole – Support

Need help?

Write to hallow@tambal.app and tell us your device, the game version (Settings screen) and what happened. We read every message.

Quick answers

No sound? Make sure the silent switch is off and tap the screen once (browsers wait for a tap). In Settings, check Sound and Music, then press "Sound check".

Lost progress? Progress is stored only on your device; it is removed when the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Game feels slow? Close other apps; on older phones the game lowers its resolution automatically.
