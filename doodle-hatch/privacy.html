Doodle Hatch: Privacy Policy

Doodle Hatch is made for children aged 3 to 8 and their parents. We collect nothing.

  • No account, no sign-in, no server.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Drawings and creatures are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them. A parent can delete all creatures in the Parent area.
  • The app makes no network requests. The only network traffic is the App Store or Google Play purchase sheet, handled entirely by Apple or Google, if a parent chooses to buy Doodle Hatch Plus.
  • Purchases and links are protected by a parental gate (a multiplication question).
