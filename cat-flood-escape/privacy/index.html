Cat Flood Escape: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Cat Flood Escape ("the game") is a puzzle game for phones. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with information.

What we collect

Only anonymous usage statistics, nothing that identifies you. The game does not ask you to create an account, does not ask for your name, email or location, and does not use advertising. To understand how the game is used and to fix problems, it sends anonymous statistics to our analytics provider: a random identifier created the first time you open the game (it is not your device identifier and is not linked to you), the game version, your phone's platform (iOS or Android), the language and difficulty you chose, when you open and leave the game, which levels you start, win, lose, quit or skip, whether you use hints, undo or other helpers, and a short technical message when the game runs into an error. Your IP address and your location are not stored, and the statistics are not combined with any other information about you. The game does not show a consent screen; this policy is the notice.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a few settings on your device only: the levels you completed and your stars, the level you played last, whether you have already seen the tips for the first levels, your chosen language, whether sound, music and vibration are on, and the viewing angle of the game board. The game also reads your device's language setting to pick its language. None of this leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Permissions

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location. It may use your phone's vibration (haptic) feedback.

Third parties

This version contains no advertising and no purchases. For the anonymous statistics described above we use PostHog (posthog.com), a service provider that processes them for us on servers in the European Union. Apple and Google, as the store operators, process your download and may collect their own data (for example download counts, or crash reports if you choose to share them) under their own privacy policies. We see only aggregate statistics they provide, such as download numbers. If a future version adds advertising, purchases or analytics, this policy will be updated before release and the store listing will reflect it.

Children

The game does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous statistics described above are not linked to any person.

Your rights

The statistics are tied only to a random identifier that we cannot link to you, so we cannot look up, correct or delete the data of a particular person. If you uninstall the game, it stops sending anything. You can also turn the statistics off at any time with the “Anonymous statistics” switch in the game’s home menu: this stops all sending and erases the random identifier from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
