Bead Jumble - Privacy Policy

Bead Jumble is a puzzle game by Yonatan Ezer. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, settings, stars, current level, booster stock and dates of the daily gift and challenge) is saved only on your device. There is no account and no server.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device advertising identifier and related technical data to serve and measure ads. In the EU and UK you are asked for consent first; on iOS you are asked for tracking permission. If you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can reopen the choice in Settings. The app is not directed to children under 13.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. The app does not receive your payment details.

Analytics

The app can send anonymous usage data to PostHog (a product analytics service) so the game can be improved: for example which level was started, won or lost, how many stars, how many pulls and how long it took, which booster or setting was used, whether an ad was watched, and which product was bought. Each report also includes the app version, the operating system (iOS or Android) and the language you chose.

These reports contain no name, email, contact details, location, advertising identifier or free text, and PostHog is told not to build a profile of you or look up your location. They are tied only to a random code created on your device, which is not derived from your device or account. In Settings you can turn the sharing off at any time (turning it off also discards reports not yet sent) and you can reset the random code, after which new reports cannot be linked to older ones. In Europe and the UK the sharing is off until you turn it on. Reports are sent to PostHog's servers in the EU. This sharing is separate from advertising: the app does not give the random code to the ad service or the advertising identifier to PostHog.
